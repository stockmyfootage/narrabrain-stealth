Privacy Policy

Compliance: DPDP Act (India) 2023

1. Data Fiduciary Identity

Narrabrain Labs Private Limited acts as the Data Fiduciary. We collect professional contact data (Email, LinkedIn Identifiers) solely for the purpose of vetting technical access requests to our proprietary Neuro-Symbolic architecture.

2. Data Principal Rights

As a Data Principal under Indian Law, you have the right to: (i) Correction of your information; (ii) Erasure of data if technical access is not granted; and (iii) Withdrawal of consent. Requests should be directed to hello@narrabrain.in.

3. Automated Safeguards

We do not use your personal data for model training or automated profiling. All vetting is performed by a human member of the Narrabrain architecture team.

4. Storage & Localization

In compliance with 2026 data residency mandates, all PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is stored on secured, encrypted local shards with zero cross-border transfer unless required for global institutional vetting.
